Vibration Test Fixture Design

Role
Mechanical Engineering Intern
Duration
January to April 2025
Team
Mechanical Engineering

Test fixture used for vibration testing for RHST; specifics of design/testing are confidential due to NDA.

The Problem

The radiation-hardened star tracker (RHST) had two potential cameras that were in consideration. My task was to develop a representative fixture that allows for vibration and performance testing of two cameras. Specifics of the design and testing results are confidential under NDA.

What I Did

Designed a fixture and led vibration testing to evaluate which camera to pursue for the RHST

  • Designed a test fixture, considering mounting interfaces, manufacturing, ease of assembly, shim stack-up, etc
  • Built test fixture in Solid Edge and ran modal analysis to ensure natural frequency is above vibration testing frequencies
  • Developed an engineering drawing for test fixture, considering tolerancing and GD&T callouts
  • Led vibration testing for test fixture, collecting data on vibration table at frequencies up until 2,000 Hz
  • Supported team in selecting which camera to pursue for RHST based on vibration/optical performance
Outcome
Findings from this work directly informed engineering decisions on which camera to pursue for the RHST. Specific results are confidential.

What I Learned

Gained hands-on experience owning a design in industry from end-to-end, from initial design to testing.
