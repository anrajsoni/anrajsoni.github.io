CAD design (left) and manufactured fuselage (right) of 2024 airplane from UBC AeroDesign.

The Problem

UBC AeroDesign is a student-led design team that builds remote-controlled aircraft for the international SAE Aero Design competition. I worked as the Fuselage Structures Lead, leading the end-to-end design of the fuselage from conceptualization to manufacturing. I managed a team of 7 undergraduate students and provided guidance in preliminary design, CAD, manufacturing, testing, structural analysis, etc.

What I Did

Being part of UBC AeroDesign for three design cycles meant that I consistently iterated on the design of the fuselage. Some key advancements I led include the following:

  • Led static stability analysis of 2024's fuselage after poor performance from 2023; considered all stages of flight during empty and fully loaded states to ensure centre of gravity always stays within 25-33% of mean aerodynamic chord
  • Led comprehensive trade study comparing different aircraft configurations, evaluating with SAE Aero Design requirements and in-house goals
  • Implemented new material choice of fiberglass for hatch of fuselage as previous iterations out of wood and carbon fiber were too heavy and interfered with radiofrequency signals; reduced weight by 300 g
  • Developed test procedures for different systems across UBC AeroDesign, notably the main landing gear; encouraged future design cycles to heavily implement physical validation protocol
  • Manufactured fuselage using various processes like laser cutting, water jetting, 3D printing, composites layup, and machining

Hatch mould process and final product for 2024 competition, made out of fiberglass.

FEA and physical testing done on main landing gear to evaluate impact/deflection.

Outcome
For the first time since 2020, our team was able to complete technical inspection and successfully fly on the runway in 2024! Our team placed 7th out of 18 teams in the SAE Aero Design competition. Beyond the outcomes at competition, UBC AeroDesign was the core project where I led a design from beginning to end. It is an electromechanical project where I had to consider electronics integration, static stability, stresses acting throughout the plane, etc.
