Oil Refrigerant Miscibility Study

Role
Hardware Test Engineering Intern
Duration
September to December 2024
Team
Hardware Test Engineering

Representative test setup shown from research paper by Purdue; actual images withheld under NDA.

The Problem

Oil-refrigerant miscibility directly affects lubrication and heat transfer performance in thermal management systems, especially compressors. Getting it wrong can mean reliability issues down the line. I supported testing to characterize this behaviour between different compressor oils and refrigerants used by Tesla under various operating conditions. Specifics of the findings are confidential under NDA.

What I Did

Designed and ran tests to evaluate oil-refrigerant behaviour across a range of temperatures and concentrations, generating data to support engineering decisions on the system.

  • Built and set up test procedures including design, manufacturing, equipment, etc.
  • Implemented RTDs/pressure transducers and developed LabVIEW program for data collection.
  • Ran structured test campaigns, collecting and analyzing performance data.
  • Presented findings to 40+ engineers to support design decisions.
Outcome
Findings from this work directly informed engineering decisions on refrigerant system design, notably which combination of refrigerant and compressor oil yields miscibility within which temperature range. Specific results are confidential.

What I Learned

Gained hands-on experience designing test methodology from scratch, instrumenting sensors for data collection and communicating data-driven findings.
