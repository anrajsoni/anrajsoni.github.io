Robot Learning Project

Class
Robot Learning: From Fundamentals to Foundation Models
Duration
February to June 2026

Video of robot picking up banana and placing it in a bowl.

The Problem

For the final project, I was required to train an SO-101 arm to pick up a banana from three different coloured bowls. The goal was to use a SmolVLM model that is trained with episodes with manual control.

What I Did

Throughout this class, I was able to apply knowledge from imitation learning exercises to allow the robot to perform a specific task.

  • Brainstormed methodologies to ensure teleoperated episodes had consistent data (i.e., position of bowls, colour of tables, etc); developed a stencil
  • Supported team in researching models to use for SO-101 arm, deciding to use SmolVLM as recommended by Hugging Face
  • Collected teleoperated episodes and tested SO-101 arm to see accuracy in picking up banana to correct bowl
Outcome
In the end, the SO-101 arm was able to perform all tasks related to picking up the banana and placing it in a different coloured bowl. Despite varying prompts (i.e., "put banana in bowl left of red"), the SO-101 arm was still able to perform. As a result, my team received 5.75 out of 6 for our final grade.

What I Learned

Gained experience in running machine learning models, specifically with a robot arm. As a Mechanical Engineering student, this course was very unique and gave exposure in learning how to train a robot with different methodologies (i.e., imitation learning, reinforcement learning, etc).
