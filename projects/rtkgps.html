Arc Boats

RTK GPS Positioning System

Role
Test Engineering Intern
Duration
May to August 2025
Team
Test Engineering

Mosaic-H Septentrio RTK GPS setup with antennas, harnesses, and boards.

The Problem

The Test team at Arc purchased a RTK GPS to benchmark performance against competitors by measuring precise data of position, heading, and speed. For over 4 months, no one on the team had gotten the RTK GPS to send or interpret signals.

What I Did

In my first two weeks, I was able to ensure the RTK GPS could telemetrize signals and interpret data through CAN bus

  • Root caused lack of telemetry from RTK GPS asset - CAN bus was overterminated with 30 Ω, rather than 60 Ω preventing signals from telemtrizing
  • Navigated Stack Overflow and suppliers (i.e., Septentrio) to find correct .dbc file for GNSS systems, translating signals into human-readable data
  • Tested RTK GPS asset against on-board vehicle telemetry, collecting data using CANalyzer and Grafana of position, heading, and speed.

Plot of RTK GPS Position Data of Vehicle Telemetry vs. RTK GPS

Outcome
This project allowed the Test team to use a new asset for measuring precise data related to position, heading, and speed. I developed a RTK GPS guide for future individuals at the company to use. This asset also assisted in an unrelated scope of work regarding station keeping (i.e., virtual anchor).

What I Learned

Gained experience in troubleshooting CAN bus systems (i.e., correct termination), using data analysis software (i.e., Grafana), and developing harnesses.
